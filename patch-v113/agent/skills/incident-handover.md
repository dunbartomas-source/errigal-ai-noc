# Incident Handover / Escalation

Use this Skill when the read-only investigation cannot safely progress or needs another owner.

Produce a compact handover containing:

- incident/alarm identity
- current impact
- controlled OEM checks completed and their outcomes
- operator observations
- current operational evidence
- relevant recent changes/timeline
- what has been ruled out
- remaining hypotheses
- relevant historical resolution patterns
- evidence gaps
- recommended next owner (senior NOC, field engineer, OEM, or other team)
- the exact reason escalation is now appropriate

Do not claim to dispatch, contact, modify, close, or send anything. Draft only.
